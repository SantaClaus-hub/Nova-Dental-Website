Our Clinical Services

From routine preventive care to complex surgical restorations, we offer full-scope oral healthcare.

1. Oral Prophylaxis

Ultrasonic scaling and polishing to remove plaque, tartar, and prevent periodontal diseases.

2. Kids Dentistry

Specialized pediatric care including fluoride applications, pit & fissure sealants, and cavity prevention.

3. Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile design, composite veneers, and aesthetic tooth contouring for a natural, confident smile.

4. Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored composite restorations designed to repair decay seamlessly.

5. Complete Dentures & RPDs

Full-arch complete dentures and flexible Removable Partial Dentures (RPDs) for missing teeth.

6. Teeth Whitening

In-office chemical teeth bleaching to eliminate tough deep-seated enamel stains.

7. RCT (Root Canal Treatment)

Painless micro-endodontic therapy designed to eliminate infection and save infected natural teeth.

8. Post & Core Build-Up

Structural reinforcement for severely broken or root-canal treated teeth prior to crown placement.

9. Crown & Bridge

High-translucency zirconia and ceramic caps to restore strength and replace missing tooth gaps.

10. Dental Braces

Orthodontic corrective treatment using metal brackets, ceramic aligners, and clear aligner systems.

11. Dental Implants

Permanent titanium tooth root replacements for fixed single-tooth and full-mouth rehabilitations.
